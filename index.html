What We Do

Full-Service Plumbing Solutions

Residential and commercial plumbing done right the first time.

⚠

Emergency Plumbing

Burst pipes, major leaks, sewer backups. We respond fast when it counts. Available 7 days a week for your urgent plumbing needs.

♨

Water Heater Services

Repair and installation of tank and tankless water heaters. We diagnose the problem and give you honest options. No upselling.

🔍

Leak Detection

Hidden leaks waste water and destroy property. We locate leaks in walls, slabs, and underground lines using proven methods.

⚙

Sewer & Drain Cleaning

Clogged drains, slow pipes, sewer line problems. We clear blockages and camera-inspect your lines to find root causes.

🔧

Gas Line Services

Gas line installation, repair, and leak detection for your safety. Proper gas work requires a licensed professional. That is us.

🚰

Fixture Installation

Faucets, sinks, toilets, garbage disposals. New construction or replacement. We install it right and clean up after ourselves.

🏠

Septic Services

Septic tank inspection, pumping, and repair. We keep your system running properly so you avoid costly failures down the road.

💧

Backflow & Water Filtration

Backflow prevention testing and certification. Water filtration system installation for clean, safe drinking water in your home.

🛠

Pipe Repair & Repiping

Old, corroded, or damaged pipes replaced with quality materials. Partial repairs and full repiping for older homes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does Veteran's Plumbing serve?

We serve Citrus Heights, Sacramento, Roseville, Rocklin, Orangevale, Fair Oaks, Folsom, Carmichael, Rancho Cordova, and Elk Grove. If you are in the greater Sacramento area, give us a call and we will let you know if we cover your location.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. Call (916) 205-7525 for a free consultation with no obligation. We provide honest assessments and fair pricing on every job.

Do you offer a military discount?

Yes. As a veteran-owned business, we proudly offer military discounts to active duty service members and veterans. Ask about it when you call.

What plumbing services do you offer?

We offer emergency plumbing, water heater repair and installation, leak detection, sewer and drain cleaning, gas line services, fixture installation, septic services, backflow prevention, water filtration, and pipe repair and repiping for residential and commercial customers.

What are your hours?

We are open Monday through Sunday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Emergency service is available. Call (916) 205-7525 any time.
