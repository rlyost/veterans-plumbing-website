## Prioritized Action Plan

### Quick Wins (do this week)

1. **Set up Google Analytics** - Replace G-XXXXXXXXXX in index.html with your real Measurement ID. 10 minutes. Impact: Critical.

2. **Claim/verify Google Business Profile** - Go to business.google.com. Add your business, verify by postcard or phone. Upload photos, add all services, set hours. This is the single highest-ROI action for local plumbing SEO. 30 minutes. Impact: Critical.

3. **Set up Google Search Console** - Go to search.google.com/search-console. Add your domain. Submit your URL for indexing. 15 minutes. Impact: High.

4. **List on directories with consistent NAP** - Make sure your Name, Address, Phone are identical everywhere: Yelp, Facebook, Nextdoor, BBB, HomeAdvisor, Angi, Google Business. Inconsistencies hurt local rankings. 1 hour. Impact: High.

5. **Ask recent customers for Google reviews** - You have 24 Yelp reviews but Google reviews matter more for Google rankings. Ask your last 10 satisfied customers to leave a Google review. Ongoing. Impact: High.

### Strategic Investments (this quarter)

6. **Build individual service pages** - A single-page site limits your keyword targeting. Each service (water heater repair, drain cleaning, leak detection, etc.) should have its own page targeting "[service] + [city]" keywords. This is how competitors are outranking you. 2-3 days. Impact: Very High.

7. **Create location-specific landing pages** - Pages for "plumber in Roseville," "plumber in Rocklin," "plumber in Fair Oaks," etc. Each with unique content about serving that community. 1-2 days. Impact: High.

8. **Start a blog** - Write 2-4 posts per month answering questions your customers ask: "How much does water heater replacement cost in Sacramento?", "Signs you need sewer line repair," "Tank vs tankless water heater pros and cons." These target informational queries and build authority. Ongoing. Impact: High.

9. **Build backlinks** - Get listed on local Chamber of Commerce, veteran business directories, Sacramento area business associations. Sponsor a local event. These links signal authority to Google. Ongoing. Impact: Medium.

10. **Create a robots.txt and sitemap.xml** - When you move to a multi-page site, create these files to help Google crawl and index your pages correctly. 30 minutes. Impact: Medium.

## Google Analytics: What to Track

Once GA4 is running, focus on these metrics:

**Weekly:** Total visitors, top pages, mobile vs desktop split, geographic location of visitors

**Monthly:** Organic search traffic trend, which search queries bring visitors (via Search Console), phone click conversions, bounce rate by page

**Set up these GA4 Events:** Track clicks on the phone number (tel: links), scroll depth on the homepage, FAQ expansion clicks. These tell you what content is working and where visitors drop off.
